Privacy policy

What this website does with information you send – aligned to how the site is built today.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy describes the static site at endemicbirds.co.tz and related enquiry channels. Safari contracts and payment processing are agreed separately in writing. Legal wording for EU/UK GDPR or Tanzania data-protection compliance should be reviewed by a qualified adviser.

Endemic Birds Tanzania ("we", "us") operates birding and wildlife safari planning from Iringa, Tanzania. Contact: info@endemicbirds.co.tz, +255 627 013 348.

What we collect

  • Safari enquiry forms – name, email, phone, travel interests, dates and free-text notes you submit on Plan your trip.
  • Email and WhatsApp – if you contact us directly, we keep correspondence needed to plan and operate your trip.
  • Operator enquiries – partnership or ground-support messages you choose to send.

We do not operate a public file upload for bird lists or passports on this site. Trip report PDFs, when published, are static downloads only – we do not track who downloads them.

How forms are processed

The enquiry form may submit to FormSubmit.co (third-party email relay) which forwards your message to our inbox. FormSubmit's privacy policy applies to that transfer. FormSubmit privacy information.

If the form fails or you prefer not to use it, contact us by email or WhatsApp instead.

WhatsApp & phone

Links to WhatsApp (wa.me) and our phone number open Meta's app or your dialler. Messages you send there are handled under WhatsApp's terms and privacy policy, in addition to how we store them for your booking.

Maps on package pages

Some itinerary pages load Leaflet and map tiles from OpenStreetMap contributors when you view an interactive route map. Your browser requests tiles from OpenStreetMap's CDN; see their privacy policy for those requests.

Fonts & icons

We load Google Fonts and may load Font Awesome from CDNs. Those providers may receive your IP address when your browser fetches assets. We do not send personal data to Google or Font Awesome beyond normal web requests.

Analytics & cookies

We do not currently embed Google Analytics or similar trackers in the codebase reviewed for this policy. If analytics are enabled later, we will update this page and any consent mechanism before tracking runs.

The site may store minimal preferences in your browser (for example dismissing the Eastern Arc sticky invite). Essential cookies from third-party embeds may apply when you use those features.

Hosting & logs

The public site is static HTML, JSON and assets. Whoever hosts endemicbirds.co.tz (for example a CDN or web host) may keep standard access logs (IP, user-agent, pages requested). We use those logs only for security and troubleshooting when available.

Sharing with partners

To run your safari we may share necessary contact and travel details with lodges, drivers, parks and insurers. We do not sell personal data. We share the minimum needed for the services you book.

Retention & security

We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as needed to manage repeat clients, disputes and legal obligations. Exact retention periods for Tanzania accounting law should be confirmed with your adviser. We use HTTPS on the live site where configured.

Your choices

You may ask what we hold about you, request correction, or ask us to stop marketing messages. Contact us using the details above. Rights under local or international data-protection law apply depending on your residence – seek professional advice if you need formal requests.

Children

Services are aimed at adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect data from children without guardian consent.

Changes

We update this page when the website or processors change. The date at the top will change when we do.
