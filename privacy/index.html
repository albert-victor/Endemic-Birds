Privacy policy

How we handle your information when you browse, enquire or travel with us.

Last updated: 14 March 2026

Endemic Birds & Wildlife Experiences Ltd. (“Endemic Birds Tanzania”, “we”, “us”) operates endemicbirds.co.tz and related booking channels. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

We are a Tanzania-based safari operator specialising in birding and wildlife journeys. Our registered office is in Iringa, Tanzania. For privacy questions contact info@endemicbirds.co.tz or call +255 627 013 348.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry and booking details – name, email, phone, nationality, travel dates, group size, interests, dietary or mobility notes you choose to share, and correspondence by email, WhatsApp or phone.
  • Website usage – standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed, approximate location) and cookies or similar technologies described below.
  • Marketing preferences – if you opt in to newsletters or follow our social channels.

How we use your information

  • Respond to enquiries and prepare tailor-made itineraries.
  • Confirm bookings, issue invoices, and coordinate lodges, parks and local partners.
  • Meet legal, tax and tourism-authority requirements in Tanzania and destination countries.
  • Improve our website and services (aggregated analytics where possible).
  • Send updates you have requested; you may unsubscribe at any time.

Legal bases

We process data to perform a contract or steps before booking, for legitimate interests in running a safe safari business, and where required by law. Where consent is needed (for example certain marketing messages), we ask clearly and you may withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing.

Sharing with others

We share only what is necessary with lodges, drivers, park authorities, insurers, payment processors and professional advisers involved in your trip. We do not sell personal data. Partners are required to protect information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

International transfers

Data may be stored or processed in Tanzania and on secure services outside Tanzania (for example email or cloud hosting). We use providers with appropriate safeguards where cross-border transfer applies.

Retention

Enquiry records are kept for a reasonable period to manage repeat clients and disputes. Booking and financial records are retained as required by Tanzanian law and accounting practice, then securely deleted or anonymised.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our site, and staff training. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; please use strong passwords and protect your devices when communicating travel documents.

Cookies

Our site may use essential cookies for security and basic functionality. Analytics or third-party embeds (if enabled) may set additional cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking essential cookies may limit some features.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, or object to certain processing. Contact us using the details above; we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority in your country of residence.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental consent.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be noted on this page.
