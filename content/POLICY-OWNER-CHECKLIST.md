# Policy owner checklist

Use with `content/policies-hub.json` (shown on [Terms & policies](../about/policies/index.html)). Draft legal pages are generated by `scripts/build-legal-pages.mjs` and marked **noindex** until approved.

## Before publishing final booking / cancellation / terms

- [ ] Confirm legal entity name, registration and postal address for all policies
- [ ] Provide deposit schedule, accepted payment methods and invoicing rules (no percentages on site until approved)
- [ ] Provide cancellation tiers, refunds, credits and force-majeure wording
- [ ] Confirm governing law and dispute resolution (Tanzania and guest home jurisdictions as relevant)
- [ ] Lawyer sign-off on all three draft pages under `about/policies/*/`
- [ ] Remove or replace `legal-draft-banner` blocks and set `robots` to indexable in `build-legal-pages.mjs`
- [ ] Update `policies-hub.json` status fields from `draft` to `published`

## Privacy (published – review when stack changes)

- [ ] Confirm live hostname and HTTPS match canonical URLs (`privacy/index.html`)
- [ ] Confirm FormSubmit.co remains enquiry processor or document new processor in privacy
- [ ] If enabling analytics or marketing pixels, update privacy **before** enabling
- [ ] Confirm retention periods with accountant / lawyer (Tanzania record-keeping)
- [ ] Professional review for GDPR / UK GDPR / other jurisdictions you market to
