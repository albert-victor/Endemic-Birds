ABOUT ENDEMIC BIRDS IN TANZANIA

We are a dedicated safari company based in Tanzania East Africa and we are leading top Birding safaris, wildlife safaris and Nature Tours in Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda. Our Birding tours have become a top choice for many first time visitors and even returning clients to this magical to our birding destinations in the regions. We also offer a wide variety of tailor-made tours, where our guests help to select the destinations and species they would love to see on their safari in Tanzania and East Africa.

Our African safari team is composed of indigenous Tour Guides drawn from Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda. These knowledgeable guides will lead you through their home region (East Africa); sharing with you their ways of life and helping you learn all forms of nature as you traverse this magical region of Africa.

We can put a tour together in just a few minutes, so you can make that last minute decision to go out and enjoy some of our best safari destinations. This has been our best tool when helping business travelers and conference attendees enjoy a tour in Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda or Rwanda. Just in case you get some few free hours during your meeting, please contact us and we shall help you enjoy an African safari within your business schedule. Kindly see our Day and Half-day Tours as well as our Excursion Tours; for some great inspiration.

Our unmatched wide range of safari destinations and tour itineraries has set us on top of the table. We take you to all the known tour and safari destinations in, Tanzania, Uganda Kenya and Rwanda. Further, we take you to exciting less-known locations that will make your African safari experience memorable. We physically visit all our tour destinations prior to taking our guests; to ensure we have the most up-to-date information on our safari itineraries. We have over 100 tour itineraries to all destinations across East Africa and we shall help you pick your best tour or safari.

We ensure you get real value for money by helping you get the best tour facilities and professional safari guides; at the best market price. We do not hold back when giving you our tour services. You get to enjoy the full knowledge of our tour leaders and guides, valuable indigenous knowledge of the local communities we visit, stay in comfortable and neat accommodation facilities, and get the most delicious meals; while on safari with us.

We understand the need to have a tour group size that will maximize the experience of each participant. Therefore, we ensure that our vehicles carry a maximum of six guests each, allowing all safari participants a window seat. When the tour group is larger than one guide can adequately attend to, we ensure we have two or more Safari Guides on the tour. This ensures that each guest is given the best tour services at all times during our tours and safaris.

We offer a varied and holistic approach during all our tours. We adapt to the needs of our guests and offer exactly what you would wish to enjoy on safari here in Africa. Our friendly tour leaders will adapt to your speed and ensure the tour moves at your pace. We also host all levels of experience, from the safari pros, holiday travellers, honeymooners, researchers, species specialists, photographers and even life-listers. This ensures that all our clients enjoy that memorable tour in all our destinations- Tanzania, Kenya,Uganda and Rwanda.
