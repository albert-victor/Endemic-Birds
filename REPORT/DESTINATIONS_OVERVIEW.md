## Data notes

- `sourceLabel` is the exact workbook category: Tanzania endemic, Near-endemic, Range-restricted, Specialty, Signature, or Your record. It is not an IUCN conservation assessment.
- `seenInPersonalRecords` mirrors source `Seen` fields only; never present it as verified public sighting data.
- `scientificName: null` means the original workbook left that field blank.
- `birdingHighlights.unmatchedHighlightText` are verbatim descriptive highlights that did not exactly match a species common name; do not turn these into additional species automatically.
- Slugs are suggested new-route identifiers. Existing live routes/slugs take precedence.
