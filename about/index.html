About

Dedicated birding
from Tanzania

We lead birding safaris, wildlife safaris and nature tours in Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda – with tailor-made routes when you want specific habitats or target species.

Our birding tours are a top choice for first-time visitors and returning clients. We also offer tailor-made tours where you help select destinations and species for your safari in Tanzania and East Africa.

Indigenous knowledge

Our African safari team is composed of indigenous tour guides drawn from Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda. These knowledgeable guides lead you through their home region, sharing ways of life and helping you learn all forms of nature as you traverse East Africa.

Time flexibility

We can put a tour together in just a few minutes – useful for business travellers and conference attendees who gain a few free hours and want a day or half-day excursion.

Varied destinations & itineraries

We take you to known tour and safari destinations in Tanzania, Uganda, Kenya and Rwanda – and to exciting less-known locations. We physically visit destinations before taking guests so itineraries stay current. We have over 100 tour itineraries across East Africa and help you pick the best fit.

Value for money

We help you get the best tour facilities and professional safari guides at fair market prices – full knowledge of our leaders, comfortable accommodation and good meals while on safari with us.

Group sizes

Our vehicles carry a maximum of six guests each, with a window seat for every participant. When a group is larger than one guide can adequately attend to, we add safari guides so service stays personal.

Adaptability

We adapt to your pace – holiday travellers, researchers, species specialists, photographers and life-listers across Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda.
